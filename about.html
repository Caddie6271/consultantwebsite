About Jason Martin

I have spent 25 years in technology, infrastructure, and security. Jason Martin Consulting is how I put that experience to work for organizations that need senior judgment without a full-time executive on payroll.

25 years 30+ certifications vCISO · vCIO · Infrastructure · Security · Web

The work has been hands-on the whole way: networks, cloud, identity, operations, and the security programs that sit on top of them. The certifications — more than 30 across the industry — are the formal side of that. The useful part is knowing which problem to solve first, and which tool or framework can wait.

Clients hire me as a virtual CISO or virtual CIO, for infrastructure and security work, and for web development when the business needs a site or portal built properly. You work with me directly. Delivery is tracked in the Work portal so requests and status stay visible.

If you need someone who has already lived through the outage, the audit, the vendor mess, and the rebuild, that is the engagement. We start with a discovery call, agree on scope, and measure progress against the outcome you actually care about.
