About

Jason Martin Consulting provides fractional executive technology and security leadership to organizations that need senior expertise without the overhead of full-time C-level hires.

We specialize in vCISO and vCIO engagements, IT infrastructure strategy and operations, and practical cybersecurity programs. Our work is grounded in real-world experience — not theory or slide decks alone.

Engagements are designed to be flexible: ongoing retainers, time-boxed projects, or advisory relationships. Delivery is tracked and transparent through our Work platform, so clients always know the status of requests, recommendations, and deliverables.

Whether you need help preparing for an audit, modernizing infrastructure, building a security program from the ground up, or simply having a trusted advisor in the room with your leadership team — we bring clarity, prioritization, and accountability.

How we work

  • 01 Discovery call to understand goals, constraints, and current state
  • 02 Clear scope and engagement model (retainer, project, or hybrid)
  • 03 Delivery through the Work portal — requests, status, and artifacts in one place
  • 04 Regular reviews and measurable progress against agreed outcomes
